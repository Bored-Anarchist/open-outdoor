# Open Outdoor Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Open Outdoor is committed to a respectful, safe, and technically honest community. Participants must:

- treat people with dignity regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint;
- discuss ideas and evidence without personal attacks, harassment, threats, or intimidation;
- give and receive technical criticism constructively;
- protect private locations, personal information, credentials, and permission-limited data;
- respect source communities, land managers, local residents, and environmental constraints; and
- avoid presenting uncertain outdoor access or safety information as guaranteed fact.

Unacceptable behavior includes discriminatory or sexualized language, stalking, doxxing, deliberate privacy exposure, sustained disruption, retaliation, impersonation, and knowingly submitting malicious or unauthorized content.

## Scope

This policy applies in repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, project events, and other spaces where a person represents Open Outdoor.

## Reporting

For ordinary conduct concerns, contact the repository owner through a non-public channel exposed by the hosting account. For security, private-data, or source-rights incidents, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not reproduce sensitive content in the report when a description or safe hash is sufficient.

Until a multi-person conduct team exists, the repository owner handles reports and must disclose conflicts of interest. A conflicted owner must appoint an independent reviewer acceptable to the affected parties where practical.

## Enforcement

Proportionate actions may include a private correction request, warning, content removal, temporary participation restriction, or permanent exclusion. Immediate removal is appropriate for exposed secrets/private data, credible threats, malware, or unlawful content.

Enforcement decisions record the applicable behavior, action, duration, appeal path, and privacy classification. Public summaries must not expose reporters or sensitive evidence.

## Appeals

A person may request reconsideration through the same private channel. Once the project has at least three maintainers, an uninvolved maintainer panel will decide appeals.
