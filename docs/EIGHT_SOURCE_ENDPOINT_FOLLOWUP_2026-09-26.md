## Remaining gates

- **Completeness:** Idaho's 27 park points and Virginia's 44 boundary polygons need park-by-park comparison; Arkansas Forestry and South Dakota Forestry still lack a confirmed direct visitor/managed-land export. A portal or map page cannot fill that gap.
- **Rights:** Idaho Recreation Trails and Virginia park boundaries carry explicit use limits. The other corrected links remain candidates until their exact license or publisher authorization covers public offline redistribution. No source was added to the shipped packages.
- **Access:** Recheck the Idaho `gis2` host and Tennessee page from a publisher-compatible client if their legacy content is needed. Do not interpret timeout, HTTP 403, or a connection reset alone as dataset retirement.
