# Redistribution rights check: Arkansas, Idaho, and South Dakota

**Checked:** 2026-09-26. **Release question:** may Open Outdoor store the data in public Git/release artifacts and distribute a processed offline app extract? This check covers the Arkansas, Idaho, and South Dakota sources in the [planned-layer tracker](STATE_DATA_PACKAGE_TRACKER.md#planned-arkansas-idaho-and-south-dakota-agency-inputs). It separates public availability, in-app use, and redistribution of source records. Rights status is per dataset, not per state or ArcGIS host.

| Dataset(s) in plan | Publisher evidence | Public Git and offline extract decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arkansas Outdoor Recreational Facilities layer 24, `owner='ASP'` and `owner='AFC'` | The [GIS Office's dataset metadata](https://gis.arkansas.gov/metadata/HTML/asdi.location.OUTDOOR_RECREATIONAL_FACILITIES_export.html) states there are **no access and use limitations**, names the Arkansas GIS Office as distributor, and credits AGFC field staff. The [GIS Office catalog](https://gis.arkansas.gov/product/outdoor-recreation-facilities/) offers the dataset for download. | **Redistribution supported by the publisher's unrestricted-use statement**, with Arkansas GIS Office and AGFC source credit retained. This determination applies to the exact facilities layer, including both owner-filtered subsets. Its 2015 campsite verification dates remain a separate freshness gate. |
| Arkansas StateParks polygons | [Service layer](https://gis.ardot.gov/hosting/rest/services/Hosted/StateParks/FeatureServer/0) credits Arkansas State Parks; [service item information](https://gis.ardot.gov/hosting/rest/services/Hosted/StateParks/FeatureServer/info/iteminfo) provides no reuse terms. | **Unconfirmed.** A public query endpoint and copyright/credit text do not grant public Git or offline redistribution. Obtain State Parks/host authorization or a specific public license. |

| South Dakota GFP Parks and Rec Areas polygons and Recreational Trails | GFP [land-service item information](https://ert.gfp.sd.gov/arcgis/rest/services/SD_Public/ReferenceLayers_LandStewardship/MapServer/info/iteminfo) and [trail-service item information](https://ert.gfp.sd.gov/arcgis/rest/services/SD_Public/RecreationalTrails/MapServer/info/iteminfo) have empty `licenseInfo`. [OSM copyright terms](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) permit copying, distribution, and adaptation with attribution and share-alike. | **Redistribution permitted conditionally under ODbL 1.0.** Preserve OSM contributor attribution and the license link in the app, document extract date/checksum, and provide any published derivative database or its qualifying change set as required by ODbL. Keep the OSM database separate from differently licensed agency records. |
| South Dakota DANR Forestry | [DANR Forestry](https://danr.sd.gov/Conservation/Forestry/default.aspx) is a program page; no DANR-managed visitor/land dataset was selected. | **No dataset to license or redistribute.** Do not transfer the GFP or School and Public Lands permissions to DANR. |

**Result:** One newly planned agency dataset family (Arkansas Outdoor Recreational Facilities) has an affirmative, dataset-specific no-limitations statement. OSM extracts have an explicit conditional license. All other selected agency datasets above lack a source-specific public bulk/offline redistribution grant in the inspected publisher material. Their status is *unconfirmed*, rather than prohibited; project noncommercial status does not resolve that missing permission. No raw agency features were committed or added to app packages during this check.

**Evidence standard:** An item-specific unrestricted-use statement, an applicable open license, or publisher authorization can clear public distribution. An empty `licenseInfo`, service visibility, a download button, an informational-use disclaimer, or a general public-records rule alone cannot. Record any later agency reply and its exact layers, permitted uses, attribution, modification, redistribution, expiry, and revocation terms before changing a status.
