# Arkansas Parks and Forestry facility freshness check

**Checked:** 2026-09-26. **Source:** [Arkansas GIS Office Outdoor Recreational Facilities, layer 24](https://gis.arkansas.gov/arcgis/rest/services/FEATURESERVICES/Location/FeatureServer/24), queried by `owner` with `fname`, `ftype`, `location`, and `last_verified_date`. These are live source counts, not records copied into this repository. The [dataset metadata](https://gis.arkansas.gov/metadata/HTML/asdi.location.OUTDOOR_RECREATIONAL_FACILITIES_export.html) states no access and use limitations; the public package decision below is about currency and coverage, not a new rights objection.

The ASP names include duplicate labels for Daisy State Park Camping Area C, Lake Frierson Camping Area, Lake Dardanelle State Park Dump Station, and West Summit Area. A repeated name may represent separate geometry or a duplicate; it must be resolved against a park map before use. `owner='ASP'` establishes the source's owner code, not that every point lies within a current State Parks boundary or is visitor accessible.

## Cross-check against agency publications

- [Lake Frierson State Park's agency brochure and map](https://www.arkansasstateparks.com/sites/default/files/2025-10/lakefrierson_06.pdf) identify a campground, boat ramp, fishing pier, playground, and pavilion, broadly matching several facility *types* in the GIS records. The brochure is an older publication despite its current file path; it does not confirm the coordinates or current status of each GIS point.
- The [Arkansas State Parks accessibility guide](https://www.arkansasstateparks.com/sites/default/files/asp_mobility_guide_1.pdf) and [park directory](https://www.arkansasstateparks.com/parks/map) provide current agency-level facility and park context, but no complete GIS point export or record-by-record match for this source was found.
- The [Forestry Division FY 2025 report](https://media.ark.org/agri/FY-2025-Department-of-Agriculture-Annual-Report_Final.pdf) reports maintenance of more than 90 primitive campsites at Poison Springs. This supports an active camping program, but the 45 GIS site identifiers and coordinates were not individually confirmed. The [Forestry camping guide](https://www.agriculture.arkansas.gov/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/L_E_Guide.pdf) describes permit rules, so these points must not imply unrestricted camping.

**Release decision:** Hold all 227 ASP/AFC facility points out of the public offline package pending record-level location and operating-status confirmation. The 2023 boating records are useful review candidates, but their age, partial park coverage, and absence of a current official point-by-point match do not clear that gate. Do not label the Forestry records as a statewide forest inventory or the Parks records as a complete amenities feed. Existing federal/PAD-US and separately licensed OSM context retains its own provenance.

**To clear the gate:** obtain current official park and forest facility exports or written steward confirmation of the specific points, identify closed/seasonal/permit-only facilities, reconcile duplicate labels and park/forest membership, and retain source observation and check dates in the package. See the [rights/contact request plan](ID_AR_SD_REDISTRIBUTION_RIGHTS_2026-09-26.md#publisher-questions-ready-to-send) for questions that can be sent to the maintainers. No agency contact was made in this audit.
