# Agency dataset redistribution rights matrix

**Checked:** 2026-09-26. **Scope:** the current registry contains 98 primary and 44 non-OSM supplemental parks/forestry source records across the 49 states outside New York. Duplicate URLs remain in both agency roles where the plan assigns the same layer twice. [New York](NYS_AGENCY_SOURCE_COVERAGE_AUDIT.md) and [OSM](CURRENT_STATE_DATASET_AUDIT.md) have separate audits. The [Arkansas/Idaho/South Dakota layer additions](ID_AR_SD_REDISTRIBUTION_RIGHTS_2026-09-26.md) include candidates beyond this registry.

**Decision standard:** “Supported” means the cited publisher terms support copying and public offline redistribution of this data, subject to stated conditions. “Conditional” means a narrower permission (for example noncommercial or processed derivatives only). “Permission required” or “Restricted” identifies express incompatible or approval-gated terms. “Unconfirmed” means no affirmative public bulk/offline grant was found; it is not a prohibition. “Lead only” is a portal, map, or information page without an exact selected dataset to package. These are rights findings, not completeness, freshness, access, or safety approval. None of the newly reviewed records was imported.

The ArcGIS item terms linked below were read from each service `info/iteminfo` endpoint. A blank `licenseInfo`, “None,” source credit, a warranty disclaimer, or a download button was not treated as permission. For non-ArcGIS sources, applicable official publisher policies were checked where found; the original page is cited where no specific grant was located. The separate [initial 143-URL validation](STATE_AGENCY_SOURCE_VALIDATION_2026-09-26.md) retains historical endpoint results; this matrix uses the current registry after the [eight-URL corrections](EIGHT_SOURCE_ENDPOINT_FOLLOWUP_2026-09-26.md).

New publisher findings in this pass: [MassGIS](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/learn-about-massgis-data) expressly permits derivative redistribution; [Minnesota DNR](https://www.dnr.state.mn.us/sitetools/data_software_license.html) permits credited modified derivatives but restricts distribution of entire datasets; [Utah UGRC](https://gis.utah.gov/documentation/policy/license/) applies CC BY 4.0 to its hosted data unless an item says otherwise; [Nevada SCORP trails](https://arcgis.water.nv.gov/arcgis/rest/services/Hosted/SCORP_NonMoto_Trails_Master/FeatureServer/info/iteminfo) and [Connecticut DEEP Property Access Locations](https://www.arcgis.com/sharing/rest/content/items/3df3c20f4f2d415a8d728525c1d6d3f6?f=json) state CC0; [NJDEP](https://dep.nj.gov/gis/faq/) requires permission before digital GIS redistribution. Alaska Forestry's publishing/credit clause was kept **unconfirmed** because it does not itself grant bulk copying. Colorado Public Domain, Connecticut DEEP Property/Trails CC0, Nebraska Park Areas no-use-limitations, California restrictions, and Virginia's no-profit condition were previously identified and rechecked here.
