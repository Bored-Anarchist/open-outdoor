# WP-205 evidence

**Status:** Implemented; local acceptance suite passed on 2026-08-31

The versioned camping evaluator applies activity, time, spatial scope, authority, specificity, supersession, conflict, and freshness rules before returning one of the seven normative statuses. Explicit prohibitions and current emergency closures remain visible ahead of positive-evidence failures. Private inholdings are excluded, designated-site uncertainty fails safely, and access is evaluated independently from camping eligibility.

Executable cases `T-UNIT-001-C01` through `C06` cover every status, inholding exclusion, designated-site behavior, stale mandatory evidence, stale prohibitions, equal-authority conflicts, supersession, and closed access with otherwise verified camping eligibility.

Verification: strict TypeScript build and `packages/data/test/camping.test.ts`.
