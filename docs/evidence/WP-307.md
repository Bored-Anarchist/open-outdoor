# WP-307 evidence

**Status:** Field-hardening states, protocol, and evidence gate implemented; physical evidence pending

The map package now reduces offline, catalog, storage, location, GPS, battery, thermal, and provisioning conditions to explicit ready/degraded/blocked states with text, icon, guidance, and per-capability availability. This keeps failures actionable and does not rely on color alone.

The binding `iphone14-ios26.6-phase3-v1` profile is recorded in `config/release.json` and its schema. Acceptance requires three 180-minute physical iPhone runs in Balanced and Endurance modes with battery ceilings of 6%/hour and 4%/hour respectively, no serious/critical thermal time, checkpoint gaps at most 30 seconds, storage growth at most 64 MiB/hour, zero post-stop sensor activity, and successful offline, recovery, degraded-GPS, and accessibility checks. The evaluator deliberately refuses simulator or replay evidence as a substitute.

Windows tests validate the state model, release-profile synchronization, thresholds, repetition logic, and evidence-source boundary. They do not constitute T-PHY-002/T-PHY-003 physical evidence; the Phase 3 field gate remains pending until signed reports from the declared device/profile are collected.

The Phase 3 guided runner now executes all local M4 suites, produces a coordinate-free physical-report template, validates a completed report against the candidate commit and binary hash, re-evaluates all physical budgets, and emits a report plus reviewer-only evidence proposal. It cannot self-accept the milestone.
