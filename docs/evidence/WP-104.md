# WP-104 evidence

**Status:** Implemented; physical elevation acceptance pending

Versioned geodesic distance and barometer-first/GPS-fallback elevation revisions record uncertainty, anchors, filter parameters, sources, and quality flags. Synthetic tests cover gaps, pauses, hysteresis, and lower-confidence fallback. The controlled physical climb remains pending.
