The five **Permission required** roles have a separate [private decision file](../config/agency-permission-required-private-validation-2026-09-27.json). [NJDEP's GIS FAQ](https://dep.nj.gov/gis/faq/) makes data available to download and use but requires prior permission to redistribute it. Three NJDEP roles are therefore staged locally with `publisherGrant: false` and `publicDistribution: false`: the parks trail role uses [State Park Service Trails layer 0](https://mapsdep.nj.gov/arcgis/rest/services/Applications/DEP_Trails/MapServer/0) because its original ZIP returned HTTP 403; the forestry role uses [Open Space layer 65](https://mapsdep.nj.gov/arcgis/rest/services/Features/Land/MapServer/65); and the forestry supplement selects trails, open-space, and POI children 0, 25, and 31 of the [DEP Trails service](https://mapsdep.nj.gov/arcgis/rest/services/Applications/DEP_Trails/MapServer). These data cannot enter public packages without a redistribution grant and product validation.

The two Michigan roles are registered in the same private status ledger but have no acquisition approval or downloaded records. The [Michigan DNR terms](https://www.michigan.gov/dnr/about/DNR-Website-and-application-policies) prohibit copying the currently linked website content without prior written permission unless law provides otherwise. Their status is `permission-needed-for-copying` until a grant or independently licensed exact dataset is available.

`node tools/stage-private-agency-resolutions.mjs` writes exact raw source files and checksum receipts only under ignored `PrivateData/agency-feeds/`. `node tools/report-provisional-agency-status.mjs` writes a 112-role private ledger. The combined state builder incorporates staged GeoJSON, while GA/TX ZIPs remain raw. No Permission required or Unconfirmed source records are added to Git or public state packages.
