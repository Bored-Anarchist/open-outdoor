## Authority and acceptance

[Project scope](../PROJECT_SCOPE.md) defines the approved product boundary. Accepted decisions refine implementation choices; [requirements traceability](reference/REQUIREMENTS_TRACEABILITY.md), [work packages](reference/WORK_PACKAGE_BREAKDOWN.md) and [test/acceptance plans](reference/TEST_AND_ACCEPTANCE_PLAN.md) retain their stable identifiers and gates.

Documents retain their own status. Archiving does not change a rights decision or turn implementation/browser evidence into physical-device acceptance. Work-package acceptance still requires a reviewer, exact commit, date and final result. Dated reports preserve observations; current generated counts and structured policy records take precedence for operations.

Changes to behavior, privacy, rights, distribution or acceptance gates must update affected specifications and evidence in the same PR. Relative links are checked by `scripts/Test-Wp001.ps1`.
